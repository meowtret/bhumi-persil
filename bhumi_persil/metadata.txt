about=Adds the BHUMI ATR/BPN parcel layer to QGIS as an XYZ tile layer. A small tile server runs inside QGIS (127.0.0.1 only), so no separate program is needed. The result is a raster image, not a shapefile, and parcel attributes cannot be clicked. UNOFFICIAL visual aid: not a product or service of ATR/BPN and not affiliated with it. The layer is for orientation only and must not be used as a basis for determining boundaries, rights, or legal decisions; use BHUMI or the official ATR/BPN services for official data. Please use it reasonably (do not pull very large areas at once). Based on the idea of BHUMI Connector ArcMap by Al Qadri.
